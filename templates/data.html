Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)

Deep learning architecture for medical image analysis

Our system utilizes a modified ResNet-50 architecture, pre-trained on ImageNet and fine-tuned specifically for brain MRI structural analysis. The network features:

  • 50 layers of deep convolutional operations
  • Global Average Pooling to reduce overfitting
  • Softmax classification layer for 4 classes (Non-Demented, Very Mild, Mild, Moderate)
  • Attention mechanisms to highlight ROI (Regions of Interest) like the Hippocampus
